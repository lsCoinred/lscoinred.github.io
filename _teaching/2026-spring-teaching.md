======

A practical training course for undergraduate students majoring in computer science. Its core objective is to take students from zero to a complete mastery of the large model training pipeline. Building on prerequisite knowledge such as deep learning and machine learning, the course systematically covers the key stages of large model development, from foundational theory to engineering practice.
