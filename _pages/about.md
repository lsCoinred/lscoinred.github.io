## Biography

I am an undergraduate majoring in Computer Science at the [School of Information Science and Technology (SIST)](https://sist.shanghaitech.edu.cn/) at [ShanghaiTech University](https://www.shanghaitech.edu.cn/), expected to graduate in July 2027. As a research assistant advised by [Prof. Kewei Tu](https://faculty.sist.shanghaitech.edu.cn/faculty/tukw/), I'm currently working on exploring extension of the syntactic language models that simultaneously generate sentences and their syntactic structures. 

I am currently interested in researches in many topics involving in natural language processing, large language model, speech processing and machine learning system. In the future, I hope to work on multilingual large language models / speech models, improving and interpreting large language models from a linguistic perspective, as well as interactive agent systems.

Beyond research, I enjoy in competitive programming contests (ICPC Silver Medalist, 2025) and spend time designing problems. I've also served as Head Teaching Assistant for several course, where I led the deployment of the online judging system and helped design course materials. These experience reinforces my own abilities while helping others gain the knowledge. 

Outside the lab, I also have some experience in embedded development and full-stack development.

**I'm currently seeking PhD / Master opportunities for 2027!**
